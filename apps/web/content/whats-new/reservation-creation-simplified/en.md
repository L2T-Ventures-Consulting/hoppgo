Creating a counter reservation took four or five steps — Customer, Period, Products, Delivery, Confirmation — chained one after the other. And once confirmed, you often had to reopen it to fix a price agreed over the counter. The customer on the phone waited through all of it.

## One page, sections

Everything is there, top to bottom: the customer, the period, the products, delivery if you offer it, and your internal notes. You fill them in whatever order suits you and go back with a glance, instead of a round trip between steps.

No more validating a step to find out what is blocking, either: on submit, Louez scrolls to the incomplete section and points at the field concerned.

## The summary is no longer a step

The old "Confirmation" step is gone. The summary stays next to the form while you fill it in: products, duration, total. It recalculates on every change, so you quote the price as you type, not three screens later.

## Pricing is settled here, not afterwards

- A **discount on the whole reservation**, from a single field.
- On each line, a **unit price**, a **total price** or a **percentage discount** — the resulting unit price appears immediately, so you know exactly what you just granted.
- The **deposit** is adjusted for this booking, without touching the product itself.

## The customer and availability too

A walk-in customer is created straight from the search field: they are saved and selected right away, and you will find them again next time.

On the gear side, only products free for the period are offered. If you decide to go ahead anyway, Louez warns you about the overbooking before you confirm, rather than letting you discover it on pick-up day.
